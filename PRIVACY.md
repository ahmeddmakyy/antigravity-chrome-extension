# Privacy & Data Handling

MyChrome operates on your local machine between Google Chrome and your local Google Antigravity installation.

- **Local Communication & No Remote Servers:** The MyChrome extension connects to the local bridge process on localhost (`127.0.0.1`) using a shared key that the helper creates on your computer (there is nothing to pair). MyChrome runs no servers of its own and collects no telemetry or analytics.
- **Data Sent to AI Models:** While MyChrome itself has no external servers, the content of pages the agent reads, screenshots, and attached files are passed to your local Antigravity agent. Antigravity sends this data to Google's AI models subject to Google's terms and privacy policy. Pages you visit may also contain untrusted text or prompt injections intended to manipulate the agent.
- **Approval Modes & Sensitive Actions:** In manual approval mode (the default), the extension requires your confirmation before using a site for the first time, executing JavaScript, uploading files, closing tabs, or clicking buttons that look risky (such as pay, buy, delete, send, or submit in English and Arabic). In automatic approval mode, these per-step prompts are skipped. In both modes, typing or sending keystrokes into password and payment card fields is strictly blocked, and navigation to non-HTTP/HTTPS URLs is blocked.
- **Protected Sites:** High-risk sites (including banks, payment processors, password managers, and major account login pages) are blocked by default in both modes unless explicitly added to "Protected sites you allow anyway".
- **File Uploads & Attachments:** The agent can only upload files you attach in the side panel and files inside the folders you list in Settings > Safety > "Folders the agent can upload from" (saved in `~/.gemini/mychrome/settings.json`). Attachments are stored locally in `~/.gemini/mychrome/uploads/` and are deleted after 7 days (checked every 6 hours). Files in your folders are never copied: Chrome reads them from disk when the website uploads them, to that website only. Paths outside those folders, the token file, directories, program files, and links or `..` that lead outside are rejected. In Manual mode every upload asks you first and shows the file names.
- **Network requests MyChrome itself makes:** the side panel asks `api.github.com` once a day for the latest MyChrome release (to show "New version available"); the installers download MyChrome from GitHub and, when Node.js is missing, Node.js from nodejs.org, and check both against published SHA-256 checksums. Nothing about you or your pages is sent in these requests.
- **Chat History & Logs:** Saved in Chrome extension local storage and on your computer in `~/.gemini/mychrome/logs/` (the log notes events and message lengths, not what you typed; it rotates at 5 MB). Uninstalling removes `~/.gemini/mychrome/` completely, including attachments and logs.
